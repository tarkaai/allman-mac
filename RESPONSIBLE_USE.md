# Responsible use

Allman for Mac is a front-end over the [`allman` CLI](https://github.com/tarkaai/allman-cli).
Everything it does on LinkedIn — reading your inbox, listening for new messages,
sending replies and reactions — goes through that binary, using your own
LinkedIn session, from your own Mac. Nothing is sent to Tarka or any third party.

You are responsible for complying with LinkedIn's Terms of Service. The CLI's
rate limits, volume caps and pre-send checks apply unchanged; the app never
bypasses them. Read the CLI's
[RESPONSIBLE_USE.md](https://github.com/tarkaai/allman-cli/blob/main/RESPONSIBLE_USE.md)
before using this for anything beyond your own conversations.
